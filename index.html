Customer information

Pay safely. Verify before you proceed.

This page provides general reminders for checking bills and making utility payments. It does not process payments, generate receipts, or request account, identity, card, or e-wallet information.

Visit Meralco’s official website ↗

Verify payment instructions

Use payment channels and QR codes only when they are provided or confirmed through the utility’s official website or verified customer-support channels.

Protect your information

Never share your password, one-time PIN, CVV, or full card details through chat, social media messages, or an unverified website.

Check your receipt

Keep the receipt issued by the payment provider. Confirm the account reference, amount, date, and transaction status through an official channel.

Need account assistance?

Open the official website directly and use the contact options published there. Avoid links sent by unknown accounts.

Quick safety checklist

  • Check the website address before entering any information.
  • Do not pay to a personal account or an unverified QR code.
  • Do not rely on screenshots as proof that a payment has cleared.
  • Contact the utility through its independently verified official channels if anything seems unusual.
